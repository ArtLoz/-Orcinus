Orcinus turns 3D models into G-code for your 3D printer directly on your Android phone or tablet, without a computer.

It is built on the open-source OrcaSlicer slicing engine, compiled for Android, so G-code sliced on the phone closely matches desktop OrcaSlicer with the same profiles.

Early preview:
• Import an STL model or add a 20 mm calibration cube
• Slice with OrcaSlicer's profiles (Creality K2 Plus in this preview, more printers to come)
• Slicing continues in the background, with progress and a cancel button in a notification
• See the estimated print time, filament use, and layer count
• A workspace laid out like desktop OrcaSlicer, following the system light or dark theme

Private by design: no accounts, analytics, or ads, and no data collected. Orcinus goes online only to check for printer profile updates, which can be turned off, and to send G-code to the printers you add.

Free and open source under the GNU AGPL v3.0. The source code is linked from the app's About page.
